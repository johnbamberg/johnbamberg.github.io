Hazard identification

The “nearly in” condition

A microphone may appear to be in the charger while remaining fractionally above the electrical contacts. This is known as performative docking.

Common warning signs include:

No positive seating The unit is perched, leaning, or delicately balanced.
No expected status indication The charger is providing feedback, but nobody has looked at it.
Optimistic departure The user leaves quickly, transferring all risk to Future Colleague.

KKindly leave it charging
Centre the transmitter Align it correctly with the charging bay.
Lower it fully Do not stop at “close enough”.
Press until positively seated A tactile or audible click is the key governance milestone.
Confirm charging status Check the charger’s expected indicator.
If it will not seat Remove it, reorient it, and try again. Do not use force, improvised tools, or prayer.
If it still fails Report the fault to the local AV support channel.

Control measure: Continue applying the procedure until “the microphone is charging” changes from an aspiration into a fact.
