WA Year 9 core focus

Linear Relations: gradient, midpoint, distance, equations of straight lines, tables of values, direct proportion and interpreting gradient/intercepts. Probability: two-stage events, with and without replacement, sample spaces, estimated probability and chance variation. Statistics: mean/median/mode/range, relative frequency, comparing distributions, sampling methods and judging whether samples are representative.
